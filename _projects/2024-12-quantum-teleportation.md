From the Einstein–Bohr debate over entanglement to its use in quantum networks.

- Builds the four orthonormal Bell states and rewrites a three-qubit system in that basis.
- Shows that when Alice measures, the state of her unknown qubit is transferred to Bob's qubit, and that Bob needs two classical bits from her to recover it. Nothing travels faster than light.
- Connects the result to quantum cryptography and the quantum internet, including Harvard's 2024 city-scale quantum network demonstration in Boston.
